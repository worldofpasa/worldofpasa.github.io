Specs are built together, not handed off.

SpecPasa gives product, design, and engineering one shared place to shape the plan. Every phase keeps its discussion, review state, and freeze gate visible—so alignment becomes part of the spec itself.

  1. 01

    Gather shared intent

    Bring rough thoughts, links, files, source references, and open questions into one starting point.

  2. 02

    Co-author the PRD

    Draft with AI or by hand while teammates comment inline against stable document blocks.

  3. 03

    Resolve the decisions

    Discuss requirements in context, close review threads, and freeze the version the team agrees on.

  4. 04

    Carry alignment forward

    Move agreed requirements into the ERD and implementation tasks without losing their history.

One shared spec. Every conversation in context.

Authors, reviewers, and engineers work from the same document. Inline threads, presence, versions, and AI provenance stay attached to the decisions they shaped—not scattered across separate tools.

Keep authorship visible—even when AI joins the team.

People decide when collaboration becomes a version. Working drafts can evolve freely; when the team takes a snapshot, human authorship plus provider and model attribution stay attached to that version.
